## Design Decisions
My website heavily focuses on the use of lines, rectangular geometry, white space,  contrast, emphasis, and proportion, to match the style and architecture used by Gio Ponti. i did this through:<br>
-the design of the shelves within the cabinets<br>
-the color contrast in the side panels<br>
-the space outside and within the side panels<br>
-the pattern in the side panels<br>
